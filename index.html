Why does Refresh Rate (60Hz vs 120Hz) change sensitivity? +

Higher refresh rate displays sample touch input twice as fast per second. Using max 200 sensitivity on a 120Hz or 144Hz screen often causes the crosshair to fly past the enemy's head, whereas 60Hz phones need higher values to compensate for frame latency.

What if the auto-scanner doesn't show my exact phone name? +

Modern mobile browsers sometimes mask exact model strings for privacy. Simply pick your Brand, Model, and RAM (`2GB` to `16GB`) in the 6-Step Configurator right below the scanner for an exact hardware match.
